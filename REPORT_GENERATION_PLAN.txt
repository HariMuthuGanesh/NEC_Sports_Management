COLLEGE SPORTS ACTIVITY REPORTS

Implemented workflow
1. Select an inclusive From / To date range in Institutional Reports.
2. Generate a report for events overlapping that period, plus all matches and
   ranked competitions scheduled within it. Include parent events even when
   their original dates fall outside the period.
3. Show a factual overview followed by event sections: dates, sport, tournament,
   registrations, activities, scores, ranked results, recorded athletes and photos.
4. Mark completed, ongoing and scheduled/postponed activities separately.
   Attendance and result records determine participation; registrations are
   explicitly labelled as event-wide totals. Distinct athletes are deduplicated.
5. Link new or existing gallery uploads using the Event selector. Reports attach
   only images linked to included events, regardless of when they were uploaded.
   Unassigned images are not guessed from captions; videos stay in the gallery.
6. Export a paginated PDF or print the report. Photos are optional. Failed photo
   downloads stop PDF export with an error rather than silently omitting evidence.
7. Regenerate after changing dates or updating events, results or gallery links.

Access
Admin / Sports President: college-wide reports.
Coordinator: relevant departmental events and activities, scoped athlete details
and competition entries; placements retain their overall competition rank.

Delivery
Backend endpoint: GET /api/reports/activity?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD
Migration: backend/src/data/migrations/028_gallery_event_link.sql
The migration is additive and preserves old uploads as unassigned. It has been
applied locally. Deploy the backend with its normal migration startup before
publishing the frontend. Link existing photos to events in Gallery Manager.
No credentials are embedded and no production data is seeded by this feature.
